# Generative AI for Business — Aviation Management

A practical portfolio of Generative AI and AI-assisted business activities completed as part of the **BBA Aviation Management** programme.

This repository demonstrates how AI can be applied to airline and airport business functions such as passenger service, communication, marketing, reporting, image generation, source verification, and management support.

## 📚 About the Project

The portfolio explores different AI capabilities and their practical applications in aviation management.

The activities focus on:

* Traditional AI and Generative AI
* AI tools and their business applications
* Prompt engineering and structured prompting
* Professional aviation communication
* Report summarisation and numerical accuracy
* Marketing copy and compliance
* AI image generation
* Canva Magic Studio
* AI privacy and safe prompting
* RAG and source verification
* AI hallucination and verification
* Human review and responsible AI
* AI-enabled aviation workflows

Traditional AI is mainly used for prediction, classification, detection, optimisation and recommendation, while Generative AI focuses on creating content such as text, images, summaries and drafts.

## ✈️ Aviation AI Applications

Examples explored in the portfolio include:

* Flight-delay prediction

* Passenger complaint classification

* Flight-delay emails and notifications
* Airline marketing content
* Passenger feedback summarisation
* Training materials and quizzes
* Airport campaign visuals
* Management reports and summaries

## 🧠 AI Workflow

A key workflow followed throughout the activities is:

```text
Business Problem
      ↓
Identify the Task
      ↓
Select AI Capability
      ↓
Generate AI Output
      ↓
Verify Information
      ↓
Human Review
      ↓
Approved Business Action
```

For aviation use cases, AI should assist professionals rather than replace human responsibility, particularly where safety, regulatory, legal or other significant professional decisions are involved.

## 📝 Prompt Engineering

The portfolio demonstrates structured prompting using elements such as:

* Role
* Task
* Context
* Constraints
* Output Format
* Tone

For example, a structured aviation email prompt specifies the flight details, verified reason for delay, required tone, output format and information that must not be invented.

## 🔐 Privacy & Responsible AI

Responsible AI practices are an important part of this portfolio.

The activities demonstrate:

* Removing personal information
* Using placeholders instead of real passenger data
* Avoiding passwords and financial information
* Avoiding unsupported policy claims
* Verifying airline information through authorised sources
* Human review before final communication

Examples of placeholders include:

```text
[PASSENGER NAME]
[BOOKING REFERENCE]
[FLIGHT NUMBER]
[TRAVEL DATE]
[FLIGHT ROUTE]
```

The safe-prompting activity specifically recommends removing sensitive information and verifying policy and status information before communication.

## 🎨 AI Image Generation

The portfolio also explores AI-generated aviation visuals using structured image prompts.

Important prompt elements include:

* Subject
* Visual style
* Environment
* Lighting
* Aspect ratio
* Intended use
* Brand restrictions
* Text and privacy restrictions

For presentation visuals, 16:9 landscape composition and realistic aviation environments were used.

## 📢 Marketing & Canva

The marketing activities demonstrate how AI can support aviation campaigns while maintaining accuracy and compliance.

The Canva campaign focuses on helping passengers:

* Verify official travel information
* Check flight status through official channels
* Keep travel documents accessible
* Follow verified baggage and security instructions
* Monitor airport display screens
* Avoid relying on unverified social-media information

The campaign also includes brand-consistency and responsible-AI checks.

## 🔎 RAG & Source Verification

The portfolio demonstrates the importance of grounding AI answers in reliable source material.

A key principle is:

> **Do not treat an AI-generated answer as the original source.**

Important claims should be checked against the relevant official document or source. The RAG activity demonstrates this using an official Air India baggage-information source and distinguishes grounded answers from plausible but unsupported answers.

## ⚠️ Responsible AI Statement

AI-generated content in this portfolio is treated as a draft or assistance tool rather than an automatically verified source.

Important aviation information should be checked against appropriate official or operational sources, and human professionals should retain responsibility for significant decisions.

### Core Principle

```text
AI prepares → Human verifies → Human decides
```
